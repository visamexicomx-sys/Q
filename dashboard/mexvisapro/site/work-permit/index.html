Digital Nomads: You Probably Do NOT Need a Work Permit

If you work remotely for a foreign employer or foreign clients and all your income originates outside Mexico, you are not required to obtain a Mexican work permit. You can live legally in Mexico on a Temporary Residency visa and continue working for your overseas employer or clients without any additional work authorization.

A work permit is only required when you earn income from Mexican sources — a Mexican employer, Mexican company, or Mexican clients who pay you from Mexico.

Who Needs a Mexico Work Permit?

You DO Need a Work Permit If:

  • You are employed by a Mexican company receiving salary paid in Mexico
  • You provide professional services to Mexican clients and invoice them in Mexico
  • You operate a business registered in Mexico that earns revenue from Mexican sources
  • You are a contractor or freelancer working for a Mexican entity

You Do NOT Need a Work Permit If:

  • Digital nomads earning income from a foreign employer
  • Remote workers with all clients and revenue based outside Mexico
  • Retirees living on pension, investment, or savings income
  • Investors receiving dividends from foreign investments
  • Permanent residents — work authorization is built in automatically

Two Types of Mexico Work Permit

Type 1: Employer-Sponsored Work Permit

Used when you are being hired by an established Mexican company or organization. Your employer initiates part of the process with INM.

  1. Mexican employer files an employer notice (aviso de contratación) with INM
  2. MexVisa Pro prepares your residency and work authorization application package
  3. You attend an INM appointment (MexVisa Pro accompanies you)
  4. Work permit endorsement is added to your residency card

Best for: Professionals being hired by Mexican hotels, corporations, NGOs, or international companies with a Mexican entity.

Type 2: Self-Employed / Independent Professional Work Permit

Used when you are self-employed, a consultant, or providing services to multiple Mexican clients without a single employer.

  1. MexVisa Pro prepares documentation of your professional activity and income from Mexican sources
  2. Application filed with INM demonstrating independent professional status
  3. Work permit endorsement is added to your residency card
  4. You register with SAT (Mexico's tax authority) as an independent contractor

Best for: Consultants, freelancers, architects, lawyers, and professionals billing Mexican clients.

Required Documents

  • Valid passport with minimum 6 months validity remaining
  • Current temporary residency card (if applying as an endorsement) or simultaneous residency application
  • Employment contract or proof of professional activity (employer-sponsored or self-employed)
  • Employer's INM notice of employment (employer-sponsored pathway)
  • Proof of professional credentials or qualifications (if applicable)
  • Passport-size photos meeting INM biometric specifications
  • Completed INM application form (MexVisa Pro prepares this)
  • INM government fee payment: approximately $150–200 USD

Frequently Asked Questions

Do digital nomads need a Mexico work permit?

No. Digital nomads and remote workers who earn income exclusively from foreign sources (a foreign employer or foreign clients) do not need a Mexican work permit. You can live in Mexico on temporary residency and work remotely without any work authorization from INM. A work permit is only required if you earn income from Mexican companies or Mexican clients.

Who does need a Mexico work permit?

You need a Mexico work permit if you: (1) are employed by a Mexican company and receive salary paid in Mexico, (2) provide services to Mexican clients and receive payment from Mexican sources, or (3) are self-employed or run a business that invoices Mexican entities. Permanent residents are automatically authorized to work and do not need a separate permit.

How long does a Mexico work permit take to process?

A work permit endorsement added to an existing temporary residency application can be processed simultaneously, adding only 1–2 business days to the overall timeline. If added to an existing residency card, the process takes approximately 5–10 business days. MexVisa Pro handles the full process on your behalf.

Can I get a work permit without already having residency?

Work authorization in Mexico is tied to your residency status. You first establish temporary residency (or permanent residency), then add the work permit endorsement. MexVisa Pro commonly processes the temporary residency and work permit together in a single procedure for maximum efficiency.

Does my employer need to sponsor my work permit in Mexico?

For the employer-sponsored pathway, yes — your Mexican employer must file a notice with INM and assume responsibility as your employer. For the self-employed pathway, you instead demonstrate your own business activity and client base. MexVisa Pro advises which pathway applies to your situation and handles all communication with INM.

How much does a Mexico work permit cost in 2026?

MexVisa Pro's consulting fee for a standalone work permit starts from $600 USD. If added as an endorsement to a new temporary residency application, it is available as an add-on from $100 USD. The INM government fee is approximately $150–200 USD. Your free consultation will provide an exact quote based on your situation.
