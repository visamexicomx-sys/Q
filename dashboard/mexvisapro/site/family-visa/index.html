Free WhatsApp Consultation
Spouses of Mexican nationals: you qualify for permanent residency directly. If you are married to a Mexican citizen, you do not need to go through temporary residency first. You qualify for permanent residency immediately — with no income or savings requirement — based solely on your marriage. This is one of the most favorable immigration pathways in Mexico.

Who Is Eligible?

Mexican immigration law recognizes the following family relationships for reunification purposes:

  • Spouse or common-law partner of a Mexican national or legal resident
  • Minor children (under 18) of a Mexican national or legal resident
  • Adult children who are economically dependent on a legal resident parent
  • Parents of a Mexican national citizen
  • Siblings of a Mexican national (in limited circumstances — consult us)

Document Requirements

Required documents vary by family relationship. MexVisa Pro prepares a personalized checklist for your specific case. General requirements include:

Spouse of Mexican National

  • Valid passport of both spouses (6+ months validity)
  • Mexican national's CURP and INE/passport
  • Original marriage certificate with apostille (if issued abroad) and official Spanish translation (if not in Spanish)
  • Proof of cohabitation (utility bills, lease agreement)
  • Passport-size biometric photos
  • INM application form (MexVisa Pro prepares this)

Parent of Mexican National

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Original birth certificate of the Mexican national child with apostille (if issued abroad)
  • Mexican child's CURP and official ID
  • Own birth certificate
  • Passport-size biometric photos
  • INM application form (MexVisa Pro prepares this)

Minor Child of Resident or National

  • Child's valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Child's original birth certificate with apostille (if issued abroad)
  • Sponsor parent's residency card or CURP (Mexican national)
  • Passport-size biometric photos of child
  • Consent of both parents (if only one parent is present)
  • INM application form (MexVisa Pro prepares this)

Dependent of Temporary Resident

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Sponsor's temporary residency card
  • Proof of family relationship (marriage certificate, birth certificate)
  • Sponsor's proof of income (supports all dependents)
  • Passport-size biometric photos
  • INM application form (MexVisa Pro prepares this)

Frequently Asked Questions

What residency status does a spouse of a Mexican national receive?

Spouses of Mexican nationals are entitled to apply for permanent residency directly — they do not need to go through the temporary residency stage first. This permanent residency has no expiration date and automatically grants the right to work in Mexico. There is also no income requirement for this pathway — the marriage relationship alone is sufficient.

Can I bring my children to Mexico as dependents?

Yes. Minor children (under 18) and adult children who are economically dependent on a temporary or permanent resident can apply for family reunification residency in Mexico. Minor children of a Mexican national qualify for permanent residency directly. MexVisa Pro processes all family members' applications together for efficiency.

Do parents of Mexican nationals qualify for residency in Mexico?

Yes. Parents of Mexican nationals qualify for permanent residency in Mexico. This pathway has no income requirement and is based solely on the family relationship. You will need to provide the birth certificate of your Mexican child (with apostille if issued abroad) and your own passport and birth certificate.

How long does the Mexico family visa process take?

For applicants already in Mexico, the in-country processing time is approximately 3–6 weeks, depending on document preparation and INM appointment availability. For those applying at a Mexican consulate abroad, the process typically takes 4–8 weeks. MexVisa Pro handles all document preparation, appointment scheduling, and INM communication.

Do family members need to prove their own income to qualify?

When applying as a dependent of a temporary resident, the primary applicant's income supports the family members' applications — dependents do not need to show separate income. Spouses and parents of Mexican nationals qualify through the family relationship alone, with no income requirement. MexVisa Pro prepares all supporting documentation to demonstrate the qualifying relationship clearly.
