What Is Mexico Temporary Residency?

Mexico Temporary Residency (Residente Temporal) is an immigration status issued by INM (Instituto Nacional de Migración) that allows foreign nationals to live legally in Mexico for 1–4 years. It is required for any stay beyond 180 days and opens the door to full participation in Mexican life.

  • Live anywhere in Mexico legally
  • Open a Mexican bank account
  • Obtain a Mexican driver's license
  • Register for IMSS public healthcare
  • Add a work permit to legally earn income in Mexico
  • Travel in and out of Mexico freely
Important: Temporary Residency does not automatically authorize you to work for a Mexican employer or earn income from Mexican clients. A work permit endorsement is required for local employment. Remote workers earning foreign income are exempt.

Who Qualifies for Temporary Residency?

  • Retirees and pensioners — qualify through pension income
  • Digital nomads and remote workers — qualify through foreign employment income or savings
  • Expat professionals — employed by foreign companies with proof of income
  • Investors and entrepreneurs — through income or investment capital
  • Family members of Mexican nationals or existing residents
  • Students enrolled in Mexican educational institutions

2026 Income Requirements

To qualify, you must demonstrate financial solvency through one of the following pathways. Requirements are set annually by INM based on Mexico's UMA (Unidad de Medida y Actualización).

Pathway Requirement (2026)
Income-based ≥ $1,620 USD/month (12-month average)
Savings-based ≥ $27,000 USD in bank (last 12 months)
Retirement/pension ≥ $1,620 USD/month pension income

MexVisa Pro confirms current figures at your free consultation — requirements are updated each January.

Required Documents

  • Valid passport with minimum 6 months validity remaining
  • Bank statements: last 3 months (income path) OR last 12 months (savings path)
  • Passport-size photos meeting INM biometric specifications
  • Completed INM application form (we prepare this for you)
  • INM government fee payment: approximately $130–160 USD
  • Proof of Mexican address if applying in-country

MexVisa Pro provides a personalized document checklist at your free consultation. Requirements vary slightly depending on whether you apply at an INM office in Mexico or at a Mexican consulate abroad.

Option B: Apply at a Mexican Consulate (Before Traveling)

2–6 weeks · Before travel

Apply at the Mexican consulate in your home country before traveling to Mexico.

  1. Gather documents in your home country with MexVisa Pro guidance
  2. Book and attend appointment at your nearest Mexican consulate
  3. Consulate issues a residency visa stamp (valid 180 days)
  4. Travel to Mexico and register at an INM office within 30 days of entry
  5. INM issues your physical residency card

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Mexico temporary residency take to process?

With MexVisa Pro, in-country applications are processed in as little as 4 business days once all documents are submitted. Applying at a Mexican consulate abroad takes 2–6 weeks depending on consulate availability.

What income do I need for Mexico temporary residency in 2026?

For 2026, Mexico temporary residency requires proof of approximately $1,620 USD/month in regular income (salary, pension, or remote work income) OR approximately $27,000 USD in savings or investment accounts over the last 12 months.

Can digital nomads qualify for Mexico temporary residency?

Yes. Digital nomads and remote workers who earn foreign income are excellent candidates for temporary residency. You can demonstrate eligibility through your monthly income or savings. If you work for a foreign employer and earn income from outside Mexico, you do not need a Mexican work permit.

Can I work in Mexico with temporary residency?

Temporary residency alone does not automatically authorize you to work for a Mexican employer or earn income from Mexican clients. You need to add a work permit (permiso de trabajo) endorsement, which MexVisa Pro can process simultaneously from $600 USD. Remote workers earning foreign-source income do not need a work permit.

How long does temporary residency last and can it be renewed?

Temporary residency is initially granted for 1 year and can be renewed annually up to a maximum of 4 years total. After completing 4 years of temporary residency, you become eligible to apply for Permanent Residency.

What happens if my temporary residency application is denied?

MexVisa Pro has a 100% approval rate across 2,500+ applications. We achieve this by thoroughly reviewing eligibility and all documents before filing — we do not submit incomplete applications. If an application is denied for a reason within our control, we will refile at no additional consulting fee.

Can I bring my family with me on temporary residency?

Yes. Spouses and minor children of temporary residents can apply for temporary residency as dependents. MexVisa Pro processes family applications and can handle multiple applications simultaneously. See our Family Visa page for full details.

Can I bring my pets to Mexico when I move?

Yes, pets can be imported into Mexico but separate rules apply — including health certificates, up-to-date vaccination records, and SENASICA (Mexico's agricultural authority) requirements. MexVisa Pro can provide guidance on pet import requirements alongside your residency application.
