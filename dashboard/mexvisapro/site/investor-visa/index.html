Investor and Business Residency in Mexico

Mexico does not have a single branded "investor visa" but provides flexible, well-defined pathways for foreign investors and entrepreneurs to establish legal residency. Whether you are relocating passive investment capital, launching a business, or expanding operations into the Riviera Maya market, MexVisa Pro navigates the right route for your situation.

  • 100% foreign ownership permitted in most industries
  • No minimum investment amount for the income/savings pathway
  • Strategic location in Riviera Maya — one of Mexico's fastest-growing economies
  • Access to USMCA (T-MEC) trade benefits
  • Favorable tax environment for foreign-incorporated structures
  • Work legally in Mexico through a formal business entity

Two Pathways to Investor Residency

Pathway 1: Income or Savings-Based Residency (Fastest)

4 business days in-country · Recommended starting point

If you meet the financial thresholds for temporary or permanent residency, this is the fastest path to legal status. This pathway requires no company formation and no minimum investment amount beyond demonstrating financial solvency.

Residency Type 2026 Requirement
Temporary Residency ≥ $1,620 USD/month income OR ≥ $27,000 USD savings
Permanent Residency (direct) ≥ $5,200 USD/month income OR ≥ $87,000 USD savings

Investors who meet the direct permanent residency threshold can skip temporary residency entirely and obtain the stronger, no-expiry status from day one.

Pathway 2: Formal Investment and Company Formation

4–8 weeks · For active business investors

For investors actively deploying capital into a Mexican business, MexVisa Pro coordinates both the company formation and the residency application:

  1. Company formation — Register a Sociedad Anónima de Capital Variable (SA de CV) or other appropriate entity with a Mexican notary public
  2. RFC/SAT registration — Obtain federal taxpayer ID numbers for the company and principals
  3. Residency application — File for temporary or permanent residency citing the investment as the basis of activities in Mexico
  4. Work authorization — Permanent residents are automatically authorized to work; temporary residents receive a work permit endorsement

Company Formation Add-On: SA de CV

For investors who want to establish a formal legal presence in Mexico, MexVisa Pro offers a company formation add-on service in partnership with licensed Mexican notaries and accountants.

  • Entity type: Sociedad Anónima de Capital Variable (SA de CV) — Mexico's standard corporate structure for businesses, equivalent to a limited liability company
  • Foreign ownership: 100% foreign-owned — no Mexican partner required for most industries
  • Minimum capital: MXN $50,000 (approximately USD $2,500) minimum subscribed capital — low barrier to entry
  • RFC registration: Company and officer RFC (federal taxpayer IDs) obtained from SAT
  • Bank account: Corporate bank account opening assistance
  • Ongoing compliance: MexVisa Pro can refer trusted local accountants for monthly SAT filing compliance
Note on restricted sectors: Certain industries have foreign ownership restrictions under Mexican law, including domestic radio and TV broadcasting, certain transportation services, and others specified in the Foreign Investment Law. Most real estate, hospitality, technology, consulting, and retail businesses have no restrictions on 100% foreign ownership.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a foreigner own 100% of a company in Mexico?

Yes. Mexico allows 100% foreign ownership of most types of companies. The most common structure for foreign investors is the Sociedad Anónima de Capital Variable (SA de CV). Certain restricted industries have foreign ownership limits, but the vast majority of business types have no such restrictions. MexVisa Pro confirms your specific industry's status at your free consultation.

Do I need to invest a minimum amount to qualify for an investor visa in Mexico?

Mexico does not have a formal "investor visa" program with a specific minimum investment threshold. Instead, investor residency is achieved through either demonstrating sufficient income or savings to meet residency requirements, or forming a company in Mexico and demonstrating active business investment. MexVisa Pro will assess which pathway best matches your investment profile.

How long does the Mexico investor residency process take?

The income/savings-based pathway can be completed in as little as 4 business days for in-country processing. The company formation and investment pathway takes longer — typically 4–8 weeks — as it includes forming the legal entity, registering with SAT, and then filing the residency application. MexVisa Pro manages the full timeline on your behalf.

What is an RFC and do I need one as a business investor in Mexico?

The RFC (Registro Federal de Contribuyentes) is Mexico's federal taxpayer identification number, issued by SAT. Any person or company that earns taxable income in Mexico or participates in a Mexican business entity must have an RFC. MexVisa Pro includes RFC and SAT registration assistance in the company formation add-on service.
