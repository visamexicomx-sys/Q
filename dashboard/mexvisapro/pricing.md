## Temporary Residency Mexico

**Price:** From $500 USD (consulting fee)
**Processing time:** 4 business days
**Validity:** 1–4 years (renewable)
**Government fee:** ~$130–$160 USD (INM)

### What's Included
- Free initial consultation
- Document checklist and review
- Application preparation and translation
- INM appointment scheduling
- Filing and submission support
- Follow-up until card received

### Requirements Summary
- Proof of income: ~$1,620 USD/month OR ~$27,000 USD in savings
- Valid passport
- Proof of address in Mexico (or consulate application abroad)

## Permanent Residency Mexico

**Price:** From $700 USD (consulting fee)
**Processing time:** 3–6 weeks
**Validity:** Indefinite (no renewal required)
**Government fee:** ~$200–$300 USD (INM)

### What's Included
- Eligibility assessment
- Full document preparation
- Application filing
- INM representation
- Card pickup coordination

### Eligibility Pathways
- After 4 years of temporary residency
- Immediate: retired (65+), high income, Mexican family ties
- Points-based humanitarian/political asylum track

## Mexican Citizenship & Naturalization

**Price:** From $1,500 USD (consulting fee)
**Processing time:** 6–12 months
**Government fee:** ~$100 USD (SRE)

### What's Included
- Eligibility verification
- Document preparation (birth certificates, marriage certificates, etc.)
- Spanish test preparation guidance
- SRE application filing
- Oath ceremony coordination

### Eligibility
- 5 years legal residency (4 years temporary + 1 year permanent minimum)
- Reduced to 2 years for: Mexican spouse/children, Latin American/Spanish nationals
