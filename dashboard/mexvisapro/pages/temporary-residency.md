## What Is Mexico Temporary Residency?

Mexico Temporary Residency (Residente Temporal) is an immigration status that allows foreign nationals to live legally in Mexico for 1–4 years. It is issued by INM (Instituto Nacional de Migración) and grants you the right to:

- Live anywhere in Mexico
- Open a Mexican bank account
- Obtain a Mexican driver's license
- Register for IMSS (public healthcare)
- Add a work permit to legally earn income in Mexico

**Temporary Residency does NOT automatically authorize you to work** for a Mexican employer — you must add a work permit endorsement, which MexVisa Pro can process simultaneously.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does Mexico temporary residency last?**
Temporary residency is initially granted for 1 year and can be renewed annually up to a maximum of 4 years. After 4 years, you may apply for Permanent Residency.

**Can I work in Mexico with temporary residency?**
Temporary residency alone does not authorize employment with a Mexican employer. You must add a work permit endorsement (permiso de trabajo) to your residency card. MexVisa Pro processes this simultaneously from $600 USD. Remote workers earning foreign income do not need a work permit.

**Can I apply if I'm already in Mexico?**
Yes — if you entered on a valid tourist permit (FMM), you can apply at an INM office in Mexico without returning to your home country. MexVisa Pro specializes in in-country applications.

**What happens after 4 years of temporary residency?**
After 4 years you become eligible to apply for Permanent Residency, which grants indefinite right to live and work in Mexico with no renewal requirement.
