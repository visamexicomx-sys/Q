## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to invest a minimum amount to get the investor visa?**
Mexico does not publish a formal minimum investment threshold for residency purposes. The qualifying factor is demonstrating sufficient economic activity or income to meet INM's financial solvency requirements. MexVisa Pro advises on the strongest documentation strategy for your specific situation.

**Can I own 100% of a Mexican company as a foreigner?**
Yes — Mexico allows 100% foreign ownership of most types of businesses. Certain strategic sectors (petroleum, electricity, media, aviation) have ownership restrictions. For most tourism, hospitality, tech, and service businesses in Riviera Maya, full foreign ownership is permitted.

**Is a real estate investment enough to qualify?**
Owning property in Mexico does not automatically qualify you for residency. However, rental income from Mexican properties can count toward the income requirements for Temporary Residency. MexVisa Pro evaluates your property portfolio as part of eligibility assessment.

**Can I hire Mexican employees with this visa?**
Once you have legal residency and a registered Mexican company, you may hire Mexican employees and comply with IMSS (social security) obligations. As the business owner with a work permit, you may also pay yourself a Mexican salary.
