## What Is the Mexico Family Reunification Visa?

The Family Reunification Visa (Visa por Unidad Familiar) allows family members of a Mexican national or a legal foreign resident to obtain Temporary or Permanent Residency in Mexico based on their family relationship.

This is the fastest and most straightforward pathway for families moving to Mexico together or for existing residents who want to bring family members.

---

## Who Qualifies?

You can apply for family reunification if you are the:

- **Spouse or common-law partner** of a Mexican national OR a legal foreign resident
- **Minor child** (under 18) of a Mexican national or legal foreign resident
- **Dependent adult child** (in some circumstances — assessed case by case)
- **Parent** of a Mexican national

The **primary applicant** (the person already in Mexico with legal status) must sponsor the family member's application.

---

## Residency Status Granted

Family members receive residency that **matches the primary applicant's status**:

| Primary Applicant's Status | Family Member Receives |
|---------------------------|------------------------|
| Mexican national | Permanent Residency |
| Permanent Resident | Permanent Residency |
| Temporary Resident | Temporary Residency |

This means if your spouse is a Mexican citizen, you can apply directly for Permanent Residency — skipping the standard 4-year temporary residency pathway.

## Required Documents

**For the family member applying:**
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Passport photos (biometric)
- Relationship proof document (see below)
- INM application form
- INM fee (~$130–$160 USD)

**Proof of relationship:**
- **Spouses:** Marriage certificate (apostilled if issued abroad, translated into Spanish)
- **Children:** Birth certificate showing parent's name (apostilled if issued abroad)
- **Common-law partners:** Proof of cohabitation and/or acta de concubinato

**From the sponsoring Mexican national or resident:**
- Valid Mexican ID (INE) or Residency card
- Proof of income or financial stability (bank statements)
- Letter of sponsorship (MexVisa Pro drafts this)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My spouse is Mexican — can I get permanent residency immediately?**
Yes. Spouses of Mexican nationals are eligible to apply for Permanent Residency directly, without going through the standard 4-year temporary residency pathway. MexVisa Pro handles this expedited pathway regularly.

**Do our children need separate applications?**
Yes — each family member requires a separate INM application and government fee. MexVisa Pro manages multiple simultaneous applications to minimize total processing time.

**What if we are not legally married — can common-law partners qualify?**
Yes. Mexico recognizes concubinato (common-law partnership) as a qualifying family relationship. You will need to demonstrate the partnership through evidence of cohabitation, shared finances, or an official acta de concubinato from a Mexican notary.

**Do foreign birth and marriage certificates need to be translated?**
Yes — all foreign documents must be translated into Spanish by a certified translator and typically require an apostille stamp from the issuing country. MexVisa Pro coordinates both services.

**Can I work with a family reunification residency?**
Temporary Residency granted through family reunification does not automatically include work authorization. A work permit endorsement can be added to your card. Permanent Residency granted through family ties includes full work authorization.
