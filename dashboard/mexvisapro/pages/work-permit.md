## Do You Need a Mexico Work Permit?

You need a Mexico work permit if you:

- Work for a **Mexican company** or employer in Mexico
- **Invoice Mexican clients** in Mexico (self-employed or freelance)
- Are a **business owner** with a Mexican company (RFC/SAT registered)
- Work **on-site** in Mexico regardless of who pays you

You do **NOT** need a Mexico work permit if you:

- Work **remotely** for a **foreign employer** and receive foreign-currency income
- Are a **digital nomad** paid by clients/employers outside Mexico
- Hold **Permanent Residency** (work authorization is included)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do digital nomads need a Mexico work permit?**
No. If you work remotely for a foreign employer and receive income from outside Mexico, you do not need a Mexican work permit. A Temporary Residency card is sufficient to live legally in Mexico. Many digital nomads in Tulum, Playa del Carmen, and Cancún operate under this arrangement.

**Can I work for multiple employers with one work permit?**
Standard work permits are tied to a specific employer or activity category. If you are self-employed or freelance, your permit covers independent professional activity broadly. MexVisa Pro advises on the best permit structure for your situation.

**Can I get a work permit without residency?**
Generally no — in Mexico, work authorization is tied to your residency status. You must have or simultaneously apply for Temporary or Permanent Residency. MexVisa Pro processes both together to minimize wait time.

**I already have temporary residency. Can I add a work permit now?**
Yes. If your current Temporary Residency card does not include work authorization, MexVisa Pro can file the endorsement amendment at INM.

**How long is the work permit valid?**
Work permits tied to Temporary Residency expire with the residency card and are renewed annually alongside it. Self-employed permits may have different validity depending on your INM authorization.
