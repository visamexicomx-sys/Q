## INSTAGRAM COLLAB POST PROPOSAL

When you have a willing partner, propose this format:

**Option A — Carousel Collab Post:**
> Co-authored post: "[Real Estate Agency] finds it. RECREA builds it. Together, we make your Riviera Maya dream happen."
> Both accounts post it simultaneously
> Both accounts comment on each other's version

**Option B — Story Takeover:**
> Spend one day posting on each other's Stories
> You show your construction process on their account
> They introduce their best properties to your audience

**Option C — Reel Collab:**
> Instagram's official "Collab" feature — one post, two accounts tagged, DOUBLE the reach
> Create a "meet your dream property's builder" or "from listing to luxury villa" Reel
> Tag each other as co-author — post appears on both feeds automatically
