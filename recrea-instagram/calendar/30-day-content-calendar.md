## DAILY STORIES FRAMEWORK (use every day)

Post 3–5 Stories daily using this rotation:

### Morning Story (8–9am):
> Project progress photo or "Good morning from [job site location]" 📍

### Midday Story (12–1pm):
> Poll, question sticker, or quiz about construction/Mexico/real estate

### Evening Story (6–7pm):
> CTA — "DM us", "Visit our website", or "Save our latest post"

---

## STORY POLL IDEAS (copy-paste ready)

1. "Are you thinking of investing in Mexican real estate?" → YES 🙌 / NOT YET 🤔
2. "Tulum or Playa del Carmen?" → 🌴 Tulum / 🏙️ Playa
3. "Villa or apartment — what would you build?" → 🏡 Villa / 🏢 Apartment
4. "Do you know that foreigners can own property in Mexico?" → YES / NO — I had no idea!
5. "What scares you most about building abroad?" → 💰 Budget / 🔧 Quality / 📋 Permits / 🌍 Distance
6. "Would you manage a construction project remotely?" → 🤔 Maybe / ✅ Yes, with the right team
7. "How long have you been thinking about building in Mexico?" → Just started / 1–2 years / 3+ years
