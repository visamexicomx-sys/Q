## HASHTAG RULES

1. **Never** use more than 30 hashtags — Instagram may shadow-ban over-tagged posts
2. **Use 20–25** per post for maximum reach without penalty
3. **Vary your sets** — using the same hashtags every post reduces reach
4. **Put hashtags at END of caption** or in first comment — both work
5. **Check hashtag health** — avoid hashtags marked "See fewer posts like this" by Instagram
6. **Create a saved collection** in your phone notes for easy copy-paste
7. **Add 1–2 Spanish hashtags** per post to reach Mexican/Latin American audience:
   - `#ConstruccionMexico` `#VillasMexico` `#InversionMexico` `#CasasDeLujo` `#ArquitecturaMexicana`
