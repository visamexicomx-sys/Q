## FILMING TIPS FOR YOUR TEAM

- **Phone is FINE** — iPhone or Samsung in landscape mode = professional enough
- **Clean your lens** before filming — a fingerprint destroys the shot
- **Golden hour** (7–8am and 5–6pm) = best natural light for construction sites
- **Stabilize** — rest your phone on a surface or use a $20 phone gimbal
- **Film MORE than you need** — shoot 3x what you think you'll use, edit later
- **Landscape + Portrait** — film both orientations so you have options
- **Audio** — if doing talking head, film indoors or away from wind/machinery

## FREE EDITING APPS (easy, no experience needed)
- **CapCut** — best free Reel editor, has auto-captions and trending templates
- **InShot** — great for trimming and adding music
- **Canva** — for text overlays and carousel graphics
