### Day 1 (1 hour)
- [ ] Optimize your Instagram bio (see Caption #1 for bio text)
- [ ] Add link in bio: construction-recrea.com/mx
- [ ] Set up Story Highlights: Projects / Process / Team / FAQ / Reviews
- [ ] Post Caption #1 (brand introduction) — this becomes your pinned post

### Day 2–3
- [ ] Post Caption #2 (before & after) as a Reel or carousel
- [ ] Start posting 3 Stories per day using the daily schedule
- [ ] Follow 50 accounts in target hashtags

### Week 1 Daily Habit (15 minutes/day)
- Like + comment on 10 posts in: `#TulumRealEstate` `#RivieraMayaInvestment` `#ExpatMexico`
- Reply to all comments within 1 hour
- Send welcome DM to every new follower

## The 3 Things That Will Grow You Fastest

1. **Reels** — Post at least 2 Reels per week. The algorithm pushes Reels to non-followers.
2. **Before & After** — This format gets the most shares and saves in construction.
3. **Collabs** — One collab with a real estate account in Riviera Maya = hundreds of new followers overnight.
