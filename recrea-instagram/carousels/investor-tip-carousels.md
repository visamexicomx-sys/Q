**SLIDE 4:**
> Mistake #3: "Skipping the soil study"
> Why it's a problem: "The Riviera Maya sits on limestone and cenotes. Building without a study = structural risk."
> Fix: "Always do a geotechnical study before breaking ground. Non-negotiable."

**SLIDE 5:**
> Mistake #4: "Trying to manage it from abroad without local support"
> Why it's a problem: "Time zones, language barriers, and distance make remote management a nightmare without the right partner."

> Mistake #5: "Choosing materials without local knowledge"

## CANVA DESIGN TIPS FOR THESE CAROUSELS

- **Template size:** 1080 x 1080px (square) or 1080 x 1350px (portrait — more feed space)
- **Slide count:** 6–9 slides per carousel (sweet spot for saves and swipes)
- **Cover slide:** Must stop the scroll — use bold headline, strong visual, clear value proposition
- **Brand consistency:** Same fonts, colors, and logo placement on every slide
- **Suggested Canva search:** "Real estate carousel" or "Construction presentation" for templates
- **Last slide always:** Clear CTA + logo + website + DM instruction
- **File format:** Download as PNG (highest quality for Instagram)
