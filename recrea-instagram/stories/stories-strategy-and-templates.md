# RECREA Construcción — Instagram Stories Strategy & Templates
## Post 3–5 Stories Every Day

> Stories keep you visible to followers even when your feed posts aren't shown.
> The algorithm rewards accounts that post Stories daily.

---

## STORY POSTING SCHEDULE

| Time | Story Type | Purpose |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **8:00am** | Project progress / job site photo | Morning visibility |
| **12:00pm** | Poll or question sticker | Midday engagement |
| **6:00pm** | CTA — DM, website, or save latest post | Conversions |
| **Optional 9pm** | Fun/personal — team, sunset, etc. | Human connection |

## STORY DESIGN TIPS

- **Brand colors:** Use your logo colors consistently (check your website for hex codes)
- **Font:** Pick ONE font and use it everywhere — consistency = professionalism
- **Canva templates:** Search "Instagram Story construction" on Canva for free templates
- **Text placement:** Keep text in the CENTER of stories — top and bottom get cut off on some phones
- **Stickers:** Always use at least one interactive sticker per story (poll, question, emoji slider)
- **No more than 3 text lines** per Story slide — keep it scannable
