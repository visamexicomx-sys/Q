**Format:** Carousel 6 slides
**Best time to post:** Monday 10am

5 mistakes foreign investors make when building in Mexico 🇲🇽 (save this before you start) 👇

SLIDE 1: "Hiring someone cheap without verifying credentials"
→ Always ask for a portfolio, references, and proof of completed projects.

SLIDE 2: "Not budgeting for permits"
→ Permits in Quintana Roo can take 2–4 months and cost 5–15% of your total build.

SLIDE 3: "Skipping the soil study"
→ In the Riviera Maya, the ground is full of cenotes and limestone. You NEED a study.

SLIDE 4: "Not having a local project manager"
→ You can't manage a construction from abroad without someone you 100% trust on-site.

SLIDE 5: "Choosing materials without local knowledge"
→ Humidity, salt air, and tropical conditions destroy the wrong materials in 2 years.
