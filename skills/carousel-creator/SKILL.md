# Carousel Creator

A specialized skill for creating high-converting, visually structured carousel posts for social media.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when the user asks to:

- Create an Instagram carousel post
- Write a LinkedIn swipe/carousel post
- Build a multi-slide educational post
- Turn a blog post or thread into carousel slides
- Create a listicle or step-by-step visual guide
- Design a before/after or transformation story
- Produce a "tips & tricks" slide series

## Workflow

### Step 1: Gather Context

Before writing, ask for:

1. **Platform** — Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, or other?
2. **Topic** — What is the carousel about?
3. **Goal** — Educate, entertain, drive follows, generate leads, or sell?
4. **Audience** — Who will be swiping through this?
5. **Tone** — Professional, casual, bold, minimal?
6. **Slide count** — Preferred number (default: 7–10 slides)
7. **CTA** — What should the viewer do after the last slide?

### Step 2: Apply Carousel Structure

Use this proven structure for high-engagement carousels:

| Slide | Role | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1 — Cover | Hook | Stop the scroll — bold claim, question, or stat |
| 2 — Problem | Agitate | Identify the pain or gap the reader has |
| 3–N-1 | Value | Deliver tips, steps, facts, or insights (1 per slide) |
| N-1 — Recap | Summary | Reinforce the key takeaway |
| N — CTA | Action | Tell them exactly what to do next |

### Step 3: Write Each Slide

For every slide produce:

```
--- Slide [number] ---
HEADLINE: [short, punchy — max 8 words]
BODY: [1–3 lines of supporting text]
VISUAL NOTE: [brief description of what image/graphic supports this slide]
```

#### Slide Writing Rules

- **Cover slide**: Must create curiosity or make a bold promise. Use a number if possible ("7 things...", "The #1 mistake...")
- **Body slides**: One idea per slide. Never cram. Short sentences. High contrast.
- **Recap slide**: Summarize in a single sentence or bullet list
- **CTA slide**: Be direct. "Save this." / "Follow for more." / "DM me 'X' to get [resource]."

### Step 4: Write the Caption

Deliver a full caption to accompany the carousel:

```
[Hook — repeat or expand on the cover headline]

[2–3 lines teasing what's inside without giving it all away]

Save this for later.

[Relevant hashtags — 5–15, mix of broad and niche]
```

### Step 5: Deliver Usage Notes

After the carousel, include:

- Recommended image style (flat design, bold typography, photo background, etc.)
- Suggested brand colors or contrast tips
- Best time to post for the platform
- A/B test suggestion (e.g., test two cover headlines)

## Carousel Templates

### Educational "How To" (7 slides)

```
Slide 1 — Cover
HEADLINE: How to [achieve outcome] in [timeframe]
BODY: Most people do this wrong. Here's what actually works.

Slide 2 — Problem
HEADLINE: The mistake everyone makes
BODY: [1–2 sentences on the common wrong approach]

Slides 3–6 — Steps
HEADLINE: Step [N]: [action verb + outcome]
BODY: [Concise explanation — 1–2 sentences]

Slide 7 — CTA
HEADLINE: Ready to start?
BODY: Save this post and follow for more [topic] tips.
```

### Listicle "N Things" (8 slides)

```
Slide 1 — Cover
HEADLINE: [N] [things/tools/tips/mistakes] [outcome]
BODY: [Teaser or provocative sub-statement]

Slides 2–7 — List Items
HEADLINE: [Number]. [Short title]
BODY: [1–2 sentence explanation]

Slide 8 — CTA
HEADLINE: Which one surprised you?
BODY: Comment below + follow for weekly [topic] insights.
```

### Myth vs. Reality (6 slides)

```
Slide 1 — Cover
HEADLINE: [N] [topic] myths — debunked
BODY: Stop believing #3.

Slides 2–5 — Myth Pairs
HEADLINE: Myth: [false belief]
BODY: Reality: [true statement + brief explanation]

Slide 6 — CTA
HEADLINE: Share this with someone who needs it.
BODY: Follow for more no-BS [topic] content.
```

## Platform-Specific Notes

| Platform   | Max Slides | Ideal Aspect Ratio | Caption Limit | Hashtag Best Practice |
|------------|------------|--------------------|---------------|-----------------------|
| Instagram  | 20         | 1:1 (1080×1080) or 4:5 | 2,200 chars  | 5–15 tags             |
| LinkedIn   | 20 (PDF)   | 1:1 or 16:9        | 3,000 chars   | 3–5 tags              |
| TikTok     | 35         | 9:16               | 2,200 chars   | 3–5 tags              |

## Quality Checklist

Before delivering the carousel, verify:

- [ ] Cover slide creates curiosity or makes a bold claim
- [ ] Each body slide contains exactly one idea
- [ ] No slide has more than 3 lines of body text
- [ ] Last slide has a single, clear CTA
- [ ] Caption hook mirrors or expands on the cover headline
- [ ] Visual notes are included for every slide
- [ ] Hashtags are a mix of broad and niche
