## Concept

Naturalis Futura bridges:
- **Naturalis Historia** — Pliny the Elder's ancient natural encyclopedia
- **Futurist speculation** — AI-generated projections of future natural systems
- **Latent space exploration** — Using AI to discover "hidden" knowledge
