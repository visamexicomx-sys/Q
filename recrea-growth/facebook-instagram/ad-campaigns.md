## Creative Specs

### Feed Post (Image):
- Size: 1080x1080 or 1080x1350 (4:5)
- Text on image: <20% of image area
- Use project photos with minimal text overlay

### Story/Reel (Video):
- Size: 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Duration: 15–30 seconds for stories, up to 60s for reels
- Add captions (85% of people watch with no sound)
- Hook in first 3 seconds

### Carousel:
- Size: 1080x1080 per card
- 3–5 cards minimum
- Last card always = CTA

## A/B Testing Plan

### Month 1: Test audiences
- Expats in USA vs. Mexico locals
- Age 35-50 vs. 50-65
- Track: Cost per lead, lead quality

### Month 2: Test creatives
- Video vs. carousel vs. single image
- Track: CTR, cost per click, lead form opens

### Month 3: Test copy
- Benefit-led ("196+ projects") vs. question-led ("Dreaming of...")
- English vs. Spanish for US Hispanics

### Month 4: Scale winners
- Double budget on winning combo
- Expand to LAL audiences
