## Post Template 1: Answer a Common Question (Value Post)

```
[Use when someone asks about construction, building, or buying land]

Title: Thinking about building in Riviera Maya? Here's what you actually need to know.

---

I've seen a lot of questions about this in the group lately, so I wanted to share some honest info from our experience building 196+ homes and commercial spaces here over 18 years.

🏗️ Things people don't realize before building:

1. PERMITS — You need a "licencia de construcción" from the local municipality. In PDC this takes 2-8 weeks, in Tulum it's been slower lately due to the growth. Plan for it.

2. ARCHITECT FIRST — Mexican law requires a licensed "Arquitecto" to sign off on the plans. You can't just bring blueprints from the US/Canada.

3. LAND OWNERSHIP — If you're not Mexican, you'll likely need a "fideicomiso" (bank trust) to hold the land legally within 50km of the coast. This is normal and safe.

4. MATERIAL COSTS — Budget has gone up ~20% since 2022 due to supply chain. Cement, steel, and labor all more expensive. Get current quotes, not estimates from 3 years ago.

5. TIMELINES — Realistic timeline for a house: 8-14 months from design approval to keys. Anyone promising less is either building fast & cheap or overselling.

Happy to answer specific questions in the comments. We're based in Playa del Carmen and work throughout Riviera Maya.

## Post Template 3: The "Is it worth it?" Post

```
Title: Is it worth building vs. buying in Riviera Maya? My honest take.

---

This question comes up constantly, so here's the honest breakdown from someone who's been in construction here for 18 years:

🏘️ BUYING PRE-BUILT:
+ Faster — you can have it in 3-6 months
+ More predictable costs upfront
- Usually pre-sale = you're trusting a developer
- Less customization
- Developer profit margin baked in (you're paying for their risk)
- Many developments stalled or delayed post-COVID

🏗️ BUILDING YOURSELF:
+ Full control of design, materials, quality
+ Usually 15-25% cheaper than buying equivalent finished product
+ You own the land outright (not shared zone or restrictions)
+ Can build exactly for Airbnb optimization (multiple studios, etc.)
- Takes longer (12-18 months typical)
- Requires more involvement upfront
- You need a trustworthy construction company (this is the hard part)

For investment purposes, BUILD tends to win on ROI if you have land or can buy land.
For primary residence with quick move-in, BUY might be easier.

The middle path: Buy land in an established area and build smart.

We've helped clients navigate both paths. Happy to chat in DMs or WhatsApp +52 984 452 5333.
