## 10 Questions PDF Content

```
10 QUESTIONS TO ASK ANY CONSTRUCTION COMPANY BEFORE HIRING THEM

(Prepared by Recrea Construction — 18 years in Riviera Maya)

1. Can you show me at least 5 completed projects similar to mine?
   (Red flag: vague photos, no details, unwilling to share references)

2. Can I contact 3 previous clients directly?
   (Red flag: "privacy reasons" — good companies are proud of their work)

3. Are you licensed to build in this municipality?
   (Ask for license number and verify at the ayuntamiento)

4. Who will be my main point of contact throughout the project?
   (Red flag: the salesperson promises one thing, then disappears)

5. What does the payment schedule look like?
   (Red flag: asking for 50%+ upfront)

6. What happens if there are delays? Is there a penalty clause?
   (Professional companies agree to penalty clauses)

7. Do you handle all permits and approvals?
   (Red flag: "that's the owner's responsibility")

8. What is included and excluded in your quote?
   (Ask for a line-by-line breakdown)

9. Who owns the materials if the project stops mid-way?
   (Should always be the client)

10. Can I visit the site anytime during construction?
    (There should never be a reason to say no)

BONUS: What does your contract look like? Can I have my lawyer review it before signing?
(Any reputable company says yes)
```
