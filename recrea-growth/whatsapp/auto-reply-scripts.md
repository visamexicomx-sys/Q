# WhatsApp Business Auto-Reply Scripts — Recrea Construction

## Setup Instructions

1. Download **WhatsApp Business** app (or use WhatsApp Business API for full automation)
2. Go to Settings → Business Tools → Greeting Message / Away Message / Quick Replies
3. Copy the scripts below

---

## 4. LEAD QUALIFICATION FLOW

After initial greeting, use this conversational flow to qualify leads:

```
STEP 1 — Understand the project:
"Can you tell me a bit more about your project? Are you looking to:
A) Build a new home/villa from scratch
B) Renovate an existing property
C) Build a commercial space
D) Something else?"

---

STEP 2 — Location:
"Great! Which area are you looking at?
→ Cancun
→ Playa del Carmen / PDC
→ Tulum
→ Another location in Riviera Maya"

---

STEP 3 — Land status:
"Do you already own the land, or are you still looking for a lot?"

(If no land: "We can connect you with trusted real estate agents who specialize in land sales. Would that be helpful?")

---

STEP 4 — Budget range:
"To make sure we're a good fit and to give you realistic options, could you share your approximate budget range?
→ Under $100K USD
→ $100K–$200K USD
→ $200K–$350K USD
→ $350K+ USD"

---

STEP 5 — Timeline:
"When are you hoping to start construction?"

---

STEP 6 — Schedule call/meeting:
"Perfect! Based on what you've shared, we can definitely help.

Would you prefer to:
A) Schedule a 30-minute video call with our project manager
B) Meet in person at our office in Playa del Carmen
C) Continue by WhatsApp for now

What works best for you?"
```
