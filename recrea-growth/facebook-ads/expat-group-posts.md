### Template 1 — Value Post (Not Promotional)

**Title:** "Things I wish I knew before building in Mexico"

```
Having worked in construction in Riviera Maya for 18 years, here are the top mistakes we see expats make when building here:

❌ Hiring the cheapest contractor (ends in nightmares)
❌ Not verifying land ownership before buying
❌ Skipping the architecture/design phase to save money
❌ Not having a written contract in Spanish
❌ Paying 100% upfront
❌ Not understanding ejido vs freehold land

✅ What works:
→ Verify land title with a notario before anything
→ Get at least 3 quotes (price AND timeline AND references)
→ Pay in milestones, not upfront
→ Use a bilingual contractor who knows local permits
→ Build a contingency fund of 10–15% into your budget

Happy to answer any questions about the building process here. We've been doing this since 2007 and have seen it all. 🙂

— Ricardo, Recrea Construction
```

### Template 4 — Myth-Busting

```
"Building in Mexico is too risky for foreigners"

Let's talk about that.

In 18 years of construction in Riviera Maya, here's what I've found:

The RISK is real — but it's manageable if you:
✅ Buy land with clear title (use a notario, not just a realtor)
✅ Use a fideicomiso (bank trust) for restricted zone properties
✅ Work with licensed, registered contractors
✅ Have a bilingual contract with payment milestones
✅ Verify permits BEFORE breaking ground

Most horror stories come from cutting corners on ONE of the above.

The OPPORTUNITY is also real:
→ Land appreciation in Tulum: 15–25%/year (past 5 years)
→ Short-term rental yields: 8–12% annually
→ USD-denominated income in a peso-cost country

We've built homes for clients from the US, Canada, UK, Germany, and more. The process works — when done right.

Any questions about buying/building here? Ask below.

Recrea Construction | 18 Years | 196+ Projects
```

## Rules for Posting in Groups

1. **Never post the same content twice in the same group**
2. **Always engage with comments** — reply within 2 hours
3. **Don't DM everyone who comments** — wait for them to DM you or ask for contact
4. **Post value first**, soft offer second — max 1 promotional post per 4 value posts
5. **Check group rules** before posting — some groups ban business posts
6. **Best posting times:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am or 7–9pm CST
7. **Track which groups generate leads** in a spreadsheet
